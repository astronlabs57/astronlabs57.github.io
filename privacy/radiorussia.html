Radio Russia

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Android app Radio Russia (package com.astronlabs.radiorussia, "the App") handles your data. In short: the App has no user accounts and no server of its own. Your favorite stations stay on your device. Data only leaves your device when the App connects to the radio stations you play (and loads their logos), through Google's advertising and consent services, and through Android's backup.

2. Data stored on your device

The following data is stored locally in the App's private storage and is not transmitted to us:

  • Your favorite stations (a list of station IDs)
  • Technical counters that control how often ads are shown (e.g. first app start, number of app starts, station switches since the last ad)
  • Your consent choices for advertising (stored by Google's consent SDK)
  • A temporary image cache of station logos

The station list itself is built into the App. The App does not keep a listening history and does not analyse which stations you listen to. We have no access to the data on your device.

3. Radio streams & station logos

The App is a player for third-party internet radio stations. When you start a station, your device connects directly to the streaming server of that station (or its streaming provider). When the station list is displayed, station logos are loaded directly from the servers on which the respective station or a third party hosts them (e.g. station websites, Wikimedia, image hosting or CDN services).

For these connections, the respective server necessarily receives your IP address and technical request data (e.g. time of the request, requested stream or image, user agent of the Android media player). Song and artist information is read from the stream's metadata and only displayed in the App. The legal basis is the performance of the service you requested (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and our legitimate interest in providing the radio function (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

The stations and hosting providers are independent third parties and process this data under their own responsibility. Many of the included stations broadcast from Russia, Belarus, Kazakhstan and other countries outside the EU, some of which do not offer a level of data protection recognised as adequate by the European Commission. A connection to a station only takes place when you play it (or when its logo is displayed). Some stations only offer unencrypted (HTTP) streams; for these, the stream content and request data are transmitted without transport encryption.

4. Background playback, notifications & Android Auto

  • Background playback: While a station is playing, the App uses a media foreground service with a visible notification so playback continues when the screen is off or you switch apps.
  • Media controls: The station name, logo and current song title are passed to Android's media session, so they appear in the notification, on the lock screen and on connected devices (e.g. Bluetooth headphones or car audio systems) that you use.
  • Android Auto: If you connect your phone to Android Auto, the station list is shown on the car's display. No additional data is sent to us.

5. Advertising & consent

The App is free and financed by advertising. We use Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland) to show banner, interstitial, app open and native ads. Native ads in the station list are clearly labelled as ads. Full-screen ads are never shown while a station is playing in the background when you return to the App. To deliver, measure and — where you have consented — personalize ads, Google processes in particular:

  • Advertising ID (a resettable device identifier)
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device information (e.g. model, operating system, language, screen size)
  • Interactions with ads, as well as diagnostic and performance data

Consent: In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the App uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to ask for your consent before ads are requested. Ads are only loaded once the consent form allows it. The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can change or withdraw your consent at any time under Settings > Privacy settings (also available in the menu at the top of the screen); if you withdraw it, ads stop loading immediately.

You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads or Settings > Privacy > Ads, depending on your device). More information: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and My Ad Center.

6. Android backup

If you have enabled Android backup on your device, Android may automatically back up the App's local data (in particular your favorite stations) to your Google account, and it can be transferred when you move to a new device. This backup is controlled by you and processed by Google; we have no access to it. You can disable backup in your Android settings at any time.

7. Permissions

  • Internet – to play radio streams, load station logos, ads and the consent form.
  • Advertising ID (AD_ID and Android ad services) – used by Google AdMob as described above.
  • Notifications and foreground service (media playback) – to show the playback controls and keep the radio playing in the background.
  • Wake lock – to keep playback running while the screen is off.

The App does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, files or photos.

8. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data through the App, please contact us.

9. Data sharing & international transfers

We do not sell your personal data and do not share it with third parties beyond what is described in this policy. Google may process data on servers outside the EU, in particular in the USA. Google LLC is certified under the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework, on the basis of the European Commission's adequacy decision. Connections to radio stations and logo servers go directly to the respective operators, which may be located in third countries (see section 3).

10. Retention & deletion

Data stored on your device remains there until you delete it. You can:

  • remove stations from your favorites in the App at any time;
  • clear the App's storage or cache in Android settings or uninstall the App to remove all local data;
  • delete the App's backup in your Google account / Android backup settings.

Data processed by Google for advertising is retained according to Google's policies. Data processed by radio stations and logo hosts is retained according to their own policies.

11. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and to object (Art. 21). You can withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future (Art. 7(3)). You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77), for example the authority competent for us: Landesbeauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit Nordrhein-Westfalen (LDI NRW), Kavalleriestraße 2–4, 40213 Düsseldorf, ldi.nrw.de. Since we do not store your data on our own servers, most requests can be fulfilled directly on your device; for data processed by Google, please also refer to Google's privacy tools.
