Charge Locator

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Android app Charge Locator (package com.astronlabs.chargelocator, "the App") handles your data. In short: the App has no user accounts. To show charging stations near you, the App uses your location only while you use it and sends the coordinates of the search area to open charging-station and map data services. Only your last known position is kept on your device (for the home-screen widget).

2. Location data

  • Purpose: to center the map on your position, find charging stations nearby, show your position on the map and calculate distances.
  • When: only while the App is open and only after you have granted the location permission. You can grant precise or approximate location; the App works with both. The App determines your position when it starts (and when you ask it to locate you) — there is no continuous tracking and no background location access.
  • Storage: the last position found while the App was open (latitude, longitude and time) is saved locally on your device, so the home-screen widget can show nearby stations without background location access. It is overwritten with each new fix, is never sent to us, and may be included in Android backup (see section 7).
  • Location provider: your position is determined via Android's location services / Google Play services (Fused Location Provider), which are subject to Google's own privacy terms.
  • Transmission: to search for stations, the coordinates of the search area (your position or a place you searched for) and the search radius are sent to the station services listed in section 3.
  • Address search: if you search for a place, the search text is converted to coordinates using your device's built-in Android geocoding service (usually provided by Google Play services).
  • Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (providing the station search you requested) and your decision to grant the Android location permission, which you can revoke at any time in your device settings. Without location access, you can still search for places manually.

4. Data stored on your device

The following data is stored locally in the App's private storage and is not transmitted to us:

  • Favorite charging stations
  • Your last known position (see section 2)
  • A cache of charging-station data and downloaded offline country data
  • Your vehicle profile (connector types, minimum charging power)
  • App settings (e.g. theme, offline countries)
  • Technical counters that control how often ads are shown

We have no access to this data.

Home-screen widget: If you add the widget, it shows nearby stations based on the locally stored station cache and your last known position.

5. Advertising & consent

The App is free and financed by advertising. We use Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland) to show banner, interstitial, app open and native ads. To deliver, measure and — where you have consented — personalize ads, Google processes in particular:

  • Advertising ID (a resettable device identifier)
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device information (e.g. model, operating system, language, screen size)
  • Interactions with ads, as well as diagnostic and performance data

The App does not pass your GPS location to the ad service.

Consent: In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the App uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to ask for your consent before ads are requested. Ads are only loaded once the consent form allows it. The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can change or withdraw your consent at any time under Profile > Privacy Settings (shown in regions where this is required).

You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads or Settings > Privacy > Ads, depending on your device). More information: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and My Ad Center.

6. Crash reports & app rating

Crash reports: To find and fix errors, the App uses Firebase Crashlytics (Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC). If the App crashes, a crash report is sent to Google containing the stack trace, device model, Android version, app version, a crash timestamp and a pseudonymous Crashlytics installation ID. Crash reports do not contain your location, favorites or the Advertising ID. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in a stable, error-free app (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Crash reports are deleted by Google after 90 days. More information: Firebase privacy information.

App rating: The App may ask you to rate it using the Google Play In-App Review API. The rating dialog is provided by Google Play; we do not receive any personal data from it beyond the public review you choose to submit.

7. Android backup

If you have enabled Android backup on your device, Android automatically backs up your favorites, station cache, vehicle profile, settings and last known position to your Google account, and they can be transferred when you move to a new device. This backup is controlled by you and processed by Google; we have no access to it. Consent and ad-related data are excluded. You can disable backup in your Android settings at any time.

8. Permissions

  • Location (precise and/or approximate, foreground only) – to find stations near you, as described in section 2.
  • Internet / network state – for station data, live status, maps, offline data packs, crash reports and ads.
  • Advertising ID (AD_ID and Android ad services) – used by Google AdMob as described above.

The App does not request background location, contacts, camera, microphone or storage access.

9. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data through the App, please contact us.

11. Retention & deletion

Data stored on your device remains there until you delete it. You can:

  • remove individual favorites and delete your vehicle profile (Profile > Delete Profile);
  • switch off offline country data to stop downloads;
  • clear the App's storage in Android settings or uninstall the App to remove all local data;
  • delete the App's backup in your Google account / Android backup settings.

Data processed by Google for advertising is retained according to Google's policies; crash reports are deleted after 90 days; data received by the map and station services is retained according to their policies.
