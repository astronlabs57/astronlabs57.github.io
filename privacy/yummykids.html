YummyKids

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Android app YummyKids (package com.astronlabs.yummykids, "the App") handles your data. In short: the App has no user accounts and no server of its own. Your recipes, favorites and settings stay on your device. Data only leaves your device through Google's advertising and consent services, Android's backup, and features you actively use (such as importing a recipe from a website).

2. Data stored on your device

The following data is stored locally in the App's private storage and is not transmitted to us:

  • Your own recipes, including photos you add
  • Favorites
  • Child profiles you create (optional): first name or nickname, month and year of birth, and allergies / foods to avoid – used to suggest age-appropriate recipes and to warn about allergens
  • Your meal plan and shopping list check marks
  • App settings (e.g. language, theme, AI features)
  • Technical counters that control how often ads are shown

We have no access to this data. It is only copied off your device if you use Android backup (see section 6) or export/share it yourself.

Allergy information in child profiles can be health data (Art. 9 GDPR). It is entered voluntarily by you as a parent or guardian, processed exclusively on your device and never transmitted to us or used for advertising. The legal basis is your explicit consent by entering it (Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR); you can delete or change it at any time.

3. Recipe import & on-device AI

Import from a website link

If you import a recipe by entering a web address, the App downloads that web page directly from the website you entered. As with any website visit, the operator of that website receives your IP address and technical request data. The App does not send any other personal data. Please refer to the privacy policy of the respective website.

Import from a photo

When you take or select a photo of a recipe, text recognition is performed on your device using Google ML Kit. The photo is not uploaded to us or to Google for this purpose. Photos are taken via your device's camera app or selected via the Android photo picker; the App does not need camera or storage permissions.

On-device AI (Gemini Nano)

On supported devices, the App can use Google's on-device AI model Gemini Nano. AI features are off by default and can be switched on in Settings. If enabled, the App can use the model to structure imported recipes. The model (approx. 2 GB) is downloaded by the Android system service Google AICore only after you confirm. Recipe processing runs entirely on your device. The download itself is a connection to Google subject to the Google Privacy Policy.

Export, import & sharing

You can export your data (recipes, favorites, child profiles and meal plan) as a file, import it again, or share a recipe or your meal plan via the Android share menu. These actions only happen when you trigger them, and you decide where the data goes.

4. Advertising & consent

The App is free and financed by advertising. We use Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland) to show banner, interstitial, app open and native ads. To deliver, measure and — where you have consented — personalize ads, Google processes in particular:

  • Advertising ID (a resettable device identifier)
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device information (e.g. model, operating system, language, screen size)
  • Interactions with ads, as well as diagnostic and performance data

Consent: In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the App uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to ask for your consent before ads are requested. Ads are only loaded once the consent form allows it. The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can change or withdraw your consent at any time under Settings > Privacy & Consent (shown in regions where this is required).

Ads in the App are restricted to content suitable for general audiences. You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads or Settings > Privacy > Ads, depending on your device). More information: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and My Ad Center.

5. Other Google services

The App loads its typeface via Google Play services (downloadable fonts). This request is made by Google Play services on your device and may involve a connection to Google servers. No personal data from the App is included.

6. Android backup

If you have enabled Android backup on your device, Android automatically backs up the App's recipe database (including meal plan and shopping list), child profiles, settings and recipe photos to your Google account, and they can be transferred when you move to a new device. This backup is controlled by you and processed by Google; we have no access to it. Consent and ad-related data are excluded. You can disable backup in your Android settings at any time.

7. Permissions

  • Internet / network state – for ads, recipe import from links and the AI model download.
  • Advertising ID (AD_ID and Android ad services) – used by Google AdMob as described above.

The App does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone or storage.

8. Children

YummyKids is a cooking app for parents and families. It is used by adults and not directed at children under 13; we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Child profiles are created by parents or guardians, stay on the device and are not transmitted to us or used for advertising. If you believe a child has provided personal data through the App, please contact us.

9. Data sharing & international transfers

We do not sell your personal data and do not share it with third parties beyond what is described in this policy. Google may process data on servers outside the EU, in particular in the USA. Google LLC is certified under the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework, on the basis of the European Commission's adequacy decision.

10. Retention & deletion

Data stored on your device remains there until you delete it. You can:

  • delete or edit individual child profiles in the App;
  • use Settings > Reset data to delete all custom recipes, images, favorites, child profiles, the meal plan and shopping list check marks;
  • clear the App's storage in Android settings or uninstall the App to remove all local data;
  • delete the App's backup in your Google account / Android backup settings.

Data processed by Google for advertising is retained according to Google's policies.

You can withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future (Art. 7(3)). You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77), for example the authority competent for us: Landesbeauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit Nordrhein-Westfalen (LDI NRW), Kavalleriestraße 2–4, 40213 Düsseldorf, ldi.nrw.de.
