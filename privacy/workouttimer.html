Workout Timer

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Android app Workout Timer (package com.astronlabs.workouttimer, "the App") handles your data. In short: the App has no user accounts and no server of its own. Your workouts, workout history and settings stay on your device. Data only leaves your device through Google's advertising and consent services, Android's backup, and features you actively use (Health Connect, sharing a result).

2. Data stored on your device

The following data is stored locally in the App's private storage and is not transmitted to us:

  • Your saved workouts (name, exercises, work/rest times, sets and rounds)
  • Your workout history: for each completed workout the date and time, name, duration, sets, exercises and an estimated calorie value; derived from it: streaks, weekly statistics and achievements
  • Your body weight (optional, in kg) – only used on your device to estimate calories burned
  • App settings (e.g. countdown, sounds, voice output, vibration, theme, language, reminder days and time)
  • Technical counters that control how often ads are shown

Body weight and the calorie estimates derived from your workouts are fitness-related data. They are processed exclusively on your device to provide the statistics you see in the App (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR and, insofar as they are health data, your explicit consent by entering them, Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR). The App does not measure heart rate or use body sensors. We have no access to the data on your device.

Export & import: You can export your workouts as a file to a location of your choice and import them again. This only happens when you trigger it.

3. Timer, voice, reminders & widget

  • Running in the background: While a workout is running, the App uses a foreground service with a visible notification so the timer keeps running when the screen is off or you switch apps.
  • Voice output: Spoken announcements use the text-to-speech engine installed on your device. Only the announcement text (e.g. exercise names) is passed to that engine. Depending on the engine and its settings, its provider may process the text; please refer to the engine's privacy information.
  • Sounds & vibration are generated locally.
  • Training reminders: If you enable them, the App schedules local notifications on your chosen workout days (including a hint when your streak is at risk). Reminders are re-scheduled after the device restarts. They are created entirely on your device.
  • Home-screen widget: If you add the widget, it shows your current streak from the locally stored history.

4. Health Connect

If you explicitly turn on the Health Connect integration and grant the corresponding permissions, the App writes each completed workout to Health Connect on your device: an exercise session (workout name, start and end time) and the estimated active calories burned. Health Connect is a component of Android provided by Google that stores health data locally on your device; you control in Health Connect which other apps may read it.

  • The App only writes data (permissions WRITE_EXERCISE and WRITE_ACTIVE_CALORIES_BURNED); it does not read any data from Health Connect.
  • Data written to Health Connect is not sent to us or to any third party by the App, and is not used for advertising, sold, or used to determine creditworthiness or insurance eligibility.
  • The legal basis is your consent (Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time by disabling the integration in the App or revoking the permissions in Health Connect. Data already written can be deleted in the Health Connect app.

The App's use of Health Connect data complies with the Health Connect Permissions policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Sharing & app rating

  • Sharing a result: After a workout you can share a result card (image and text with workout name, duration, exercises and calories) via the Android share sheet. The data is only passed to the app you choose, and only when you trigger it.
  • App rating: The App may ask you to rate it using the Google Play In-App Review API. The rating dialog is provided by Google Play; we do not receive any personal data from it beyond the public review you choose to submit.

6. Advertising & consent

The App is free and financed by advertising. We use Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland) to show banner, interstitial, app open and native ads. To deliver, measure and — where you have consented — personalize ads, Google processes in particular:

  • Advertising ID (a resettable device identifier)
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device information (e.g. model, operating system, language, screen size)
  • Interactions with ads, as well as diagnostic and performance data

Consent: In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the App uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to ask for your consent before ads are requested. Ads are only loaded once the consent form allows it. The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can change or withdraw your consent at any time under Settings > Privacy Settings; if you withdraw it, ads stop loading immediately.

You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads or Settings > Privacy > Ads, depending on your device). More information: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and My Ad Center.

7. Android backup

If you have enabled Android backup on your device, Android automatically backs up your saved workouts, workout history, body weight and settings to your Google account, and they can be transferred when you move to a new device. This backup is controlled by you and processed by Google; we have no access to it. Consent and ad-related data are excluded. You can disable backup in your Android settings at any time.

8. Permissions

  • Internet / network state – only for ads and the consent form.
  • Advertising ID (AD_ID and Android ad services) – used by Google AdMob as described above.
  • Notifications and foreground service – to show the running timer and keep it active in the background, and for training reminders.
  • Run at startup (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED) – to restore scheduled training reminders after a device restart.
  • Health Connect (WRITE_EXERCISE, WRITE_ACTIVE_CALORIES_BURNED) – optional, only after you grant them; see section 4.
  • Wake lock – to keep the timer accurate while the screen is off.
  • Vibration – for haptic signals.

The App does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, body sensors or physical activity recognition, and does not read data from Health Connect.

9. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data through the App, please contact us.

10. Data sharing & international transfers

We do not sell your personal data and do not share it with third parties beyond what is described in this policy. Google may process data on servers outside the EU, in particular in the USA. Google LLC is certified under the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework, on the basis of the European Commission's adequacy decision.

11. Retention & deletion

Data stored on your device remains there until you delete it. You can:

  • delete individual workouts in the App (Delete Workout);
  • delete individual history entries or the entire workout history (Clear History);
  • delete data written to Health Connect in the Health Connect app;
  • clear the App's storage in Android settings or uninstall the App to remove all local data;
  • delete the App's backup in your Google account / Android backup settings.

Data processed by Google for advertising is retained according to Google's policies.
